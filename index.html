Common questions.

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp, a real person replies during working hours.

Ask on WhatsApp
How long does a project take?+
A logo takes 3–5 days, a full brand identity 1–2 weeks, a website 7–10 days and a mobile app design 10–15 days. Shoot and video dates are agreed with you. You get a written timeline before we start.
How much does it cost?+
Websites, videos, design and social media are quoted after a free 15-minute call, so you only pay for what you need. Print has fixed prices, like visiting cards from ₹3 each. Get a quote →
Do you shoot and edit videos?+
Yes. Book a single shoot day or a single edit, or choose a monthly plan that covers shoots, reels and posting. See plans →
Are revisions included?+
Always. The number of rounds depends on the package and is agreed upfront, so there are no surprises.
Do you work outside Bengaluru?+
Yes. We're based in HSR Layout, Bengaluru with roots in Madanapalle, and websites, design and editing happen fully online. We also deliver print and gifts across India.
What do you need from me to start?+
A short brief about your business and audience, any references you like, and existing assets (logo, colours, fonts). We send a simple questionnaire to make this easy.
