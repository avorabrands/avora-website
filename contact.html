How long does a project take?
A logo takes 3–5 days, a full brand identity 1–2 weeks, a website 7–10 days and a mobile app design 10–15 days. Print timelines depend on the product and are confirmed with your proof. You get a written timeline before we start.
How much does it cost?
Popular print items have published prices (visiting cards from ₹3 each, letterheads from ₹19). Design is quoted per project after a free 15-minute call, so you only pay for what you need.
Can I order gift hampers in bulk with my logo?
Yes. We brand the box, card, ribbon and products, and deliver to the address you share with us. See gifts and merch
Are revisions included?
Always. The number of rounds depends on the package and is agreed upfront, so there are no surprises.
Do you work with startups and small businesses?
Yes, most of our clients are. Packages are flexible.
Do you work and deliver outside Bengaluru?
Yes. We’re based in HSR Layout, Bengaluru with roots in Madanapalle, and we deliver print and gifts across India. Design work happens fully online.
