How ordering works

There is no online checkout. Your list is a request for a quote, and nothing is ordered or charged until you confirm it.

  1. Browse the catalogue

    Pick a category and see minimum quantities.

  2. Build a list

    Add products and set how many you need.

  3. Request a quote

    Send the list from this site or on WhatsApp. We reply with prices, samples and a delivery date.

  4. Approve and receive

    Share your file or we design it. Nothing prints without your OK on the proof.

Print questions

How fast can I get my order?
It depends on the product, the quantity and your delivery location. Before we start, we discuss your order with you and fix a delivery date together. Need it urgently? Tell us and we will plan around it.
What file should I send?
A print-ready PDF in CMYK at 300 DPI with 3 mm bleed is ideal. Don’t worry if you don’t have one, we check and fix files for free.
Is there a minimum quantity?
It depends on the product and is shown on each one. Bulk orders get better per-unit pricing.
Can I see a sample before a bulk order?
Ask when you request a quote. For many products we can share a sample or a physical proof before the full run.

Tell us what you’re printing.

Not in the catalogue, or not sure which finish? Describe it and we’ll suggest options.
