Why Avora exists

“Your brand speaks before you do. We started Avora so every business, not just the big ones, gets a brand that earns trust before the first conversation.”

Reddy Pavan M, Founder & CEO

As a UI/UX designer, our founder Reddy Pavan M kept seeing the same thing: good businesses losing customers because their brand didn’t look as trustworthy as they actually were.

The fix was rarely just a better logo. Owners were juggling a freelancer for design, a print shop for cards and someone else for the website, and nothing looked like it came from the same company.

So we built the studio we wished existed. Reddy Pavan M is the founder and CEO. Namratha E and Praveen Devarakonda are co-founders: Namratha leads design, Praveen leads the technical side. Vignesh manages quotes, timelines and client care.

Today the team designs brands, apps and magazines, produces everything from visiting cards to gift hampers, and delivers it, for FinTech startups, clinics, Telugu publishers and local businesses.
